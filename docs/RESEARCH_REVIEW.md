# Pre-publication research review

Before sharing a map, table, or conclusion publicly, confirm the following.

## Data identity and coverage

- Can each input be traced to a product version and source granule?
- Is the time range supported by actual valid observations?
- Are coverage gaps and quality exclusions visible?
- Is the study boundary appropriate for the claim?

## Methods

- Are units, scaling, quality masks, and anomaly baseline documented?
- Is the lag convention clear to a reader?
- Are estimates accompanied by valid sample counts?
- Are serial dependence and multiple comparisons addressed?
- Are alternative choices or sensitivity tests reported?

## Interpretation

- Does the text distinguish correlation from causation?
- Are uncertainty and limitations described?
- Are selected lags presented as estimates rather than universal ecological truths?
- Are synthetic examples excluded from observational conclusions?

## Reproducibility

- Is the code revision recorded?
- Are environment and input metadata preserved?
- Can another person reproduce the published artifact?
- Does the output include a manifest and checksum?

If a required answer is unknown, disclose it and avoid overstating the finding.

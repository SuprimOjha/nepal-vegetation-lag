# Research questions and analysis plan

## Primary question

Across Nepal, at what monthly offset is the association between precipitation anomalies and vegetation-index anomalies strongest, and how does that estimate vary spatially?

## Supporting questions

1. Does the estimated lag vary across seasons or ecological/elevation zones?
2. Which locations have enough valid observations to support an estimate?
3. How sensitive are results to anomaly baseline, quality masking, grid resolution, and lag range?
4. Do estimated associations remain credible after accounting for temporal autocorrelation and multiple testing?

## Hypothesis discipline

These are questions to test, not conclusions. The analysis must not assume a particular lag or direction in advance. Report effect sizes, uncertainty, sample counts, and sensitivity checks alongside any selected lag.

## Scope boundaries

The initial workflow focuses on monthly precipitation and vegetation data. A lag-correlation map alone cannot establish causality, diagnose a specific drought, or prove a land-management mechanism. Those claims require additional evidence and a suitable research design.
